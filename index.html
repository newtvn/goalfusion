About GoalFusion

Kitengela's First
Multi-Brand Complex

GoalFusion is the digital home of the GoalHub Complex — a first-of-its-kind lifestyle destination in Kitengela that brings together world-class sport facilities, authentic local food, and professional home-care services under one roof.

Whether you're booking a football pitch with your crew, grabbing a hearty meal after the game, or getting your carpets freshened up before the weekend — everything you need is right here. One location. Three brands. Zero hassle.

GoalFusion is more than a hub — it's the heartbeat of the Kitengela community.

⚽

GoalHub — Sports & Turf

Professional 5-a-side and 7-a-side football turfs in Kitengela. Book online, join leagues, and host tournaments at Kitengela's most popular football complex.

🍽

Kienyeji Hub — Restaurant

Rooted in Kenyan tradition, Kienyeji Hub serves genuine home-style cooking — from nyama choma to pilau — in a warm, relaxed atmosphere right next to the pitch.

🧺

ExpressCarpets — Home Care

Kenya's leading carpet and fabric cleaning service. We collect from your door, deep-clean with industrial equipment, and return same-day or next-day.
