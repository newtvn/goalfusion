Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I book a football pitch?

Visit the GoalHub page and select your preferred date, time, and pitch size. You can book online 24/7 or call our bookings line.

Does Kienyeji Hub take reservations?

Yes! You can reserve a table by calling us or using the booking form on the Kienyeji Hub page. Walk-ins are also welcome.

How does ExpressCarpets pickup work?

Schedule a pickup online or by phone. We collect from your doorstep, deep-clean at our facility, and deliver back — usually same-day or next-day.

Is there parking available?

Yes. The GoalHub Complex has ample on-site parking available free of charge for all customers and visitors.

Can I host a private event?

Absolutely. We offer event hosting across all three brands — corporate team-building at GoalHub, private dining at Kienyeji Hub, and more. Contact us for packages.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, and cash across all our services. Online bookings support M-Pesa and card payments.
