Common Questions

FAQs

Can I book same day?

Yes — for GoalHub pitches and ExpressCarpets (where slots are available). Walk-ins are always welcome at Kienyeji Hub.

Do you accept M-Pesa?

Absolutely. We accept M-Pesa, card, and cash at the facility. Online bookings use M-Pesa or card.

Where exactly are you located?

We're on Namanga Road in Kitengela, easily accessible from Nairobi. Look for the GoalHub Complex signage.

Is parking available?

Yes — free parking is available at the GoalHub Complex for all visitors, including Kienyeji Hub and ExpressCarpets customers.
