Our Story

Born from a Simple Idea:
Everything You Need, Here.

GoalFusion started with a question: why should the people of Kitengela have to drive across town for quality sport, great food, and reliable home care? The answer was simple — they shouldn't.

The GoalHub Complex was born at the intersection of community and convenience. We brought together three independent brands, each exceptional at what they do, and put them in the same place so you can get more done in less time.

Today GoalFusion is more than a complex — it's the social and commercial heartbeat of Kitengela. From the pitch to the plate to your doorstep, we're here every day of the week.
